Common questions

What video angle should I use?

A forward-facing onboard angle is best. Helmet, chest, or kart-mounted footage can all work if the track and steering inputs are clear.

How long should the video be?

One clear session or race clip is enough to start. Quality beats quantity.

How long does feedback take?

The first version will be handled manually. Turnaround time will be confirmed when the coaching offer goes live.

Is this for beginners or racers?

Both. The feedback is matched to the driver's current level and next practical step.
